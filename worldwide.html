BERENSTAIN / BERENSTEIN

GLOBAL // reported 1980s–now TEXT

An enormous number of people distinctly remember the children's books as "Berenstein". Every copy now reads "Berenstain". The most common single example people cite. Ask yourself before you look it up.

MONOPOLY MAN'S MONOCLE

GLOBAL IMAGE

Rich Uncle Pennybags — the Monopoly mascot — is remembered by millions as wearing a monocle. He does not, and reportedly never did. The Planters peanut, however, does. Something got swapped.

FRUIT OF THE LOOM CORNUCOPIA

GLOBAL LOGO

The clothing logo: a pile of fruit spilling from a brown horn of plenty — a cornucopia. There is no cornucopia. There never was, according to the record. Yet the memory is specific and shared.

NEW ZEALAND HAS MOVED

GLOBAL MAP

A large group of people remember New Zealand sitting to the north-east of Australia. On current maps it sits to the south-east. People are re-learning the geography of a country in real time.

"LUKE, I AM YOUR FATHER"

GLOBAL QUOTE

The most quoted line in film history. The film does not contain it — the actual line is "No, I am your father." Everyone remembers the version that was never said.

SINBAD'S GENIE MOVIE ("SHAZAAM")

GLOBAL FILM

Thousands recall a 1990s film where the comedian Sinbad played a genie. No such film is on record. People describe the poster, the plot, specific scenes. It is not supposed to exist.

GLOBAL RECORD
