SPIRE COLOUR CHANGE

2003-11-04 // 03:14 LIGHT

Passed the Spire on the way home. For about ninety seconds it was clearly casting a gold-violet light, not white. Two other people stopped. By the time I got my phone out it was "normal" again. Photo is soft but the colour is there.

MURAL SHIFT

2003-11-06 // 14:22 MEMORY

The gable-end mural off the main road. I've walked past it my whole life. Today the figure on the left is facing the other way and the year painted underneath is wrong. Colours look smeared, like they were repainted wet. Nobody at the shop remembers it being different.

RAVEN CLUSTER — DOCKS

2003-11-09 // 06:48 FAUNA

40+ ravens on the dock cranes before sunrise. Completely silent. They were all facing the water. They stayed exactly like this for the twenty minutes I watched. Then, together, gone. No sound the whole time.

GLYPH SIGHTING

2003-11-12 // 23:57 SYMBOL

Same angular symbol I've now seen in three different places across the city — always low on wet concrete, always faintly gold. It is not graffiti I recognise. If you know what this is, tell me.

BELFAST MEMORY SLIP

2003-11-18 // 19:30 MEMORY

Four separate people told me a street name today. All four were confident. All four were different. I've lived here thirty years and for a second I couldn't picture which end of the road the chippy is on. It came back. But it left first. That's new.
